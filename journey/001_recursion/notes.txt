combining method
there is a:
	- base case
	  the piece that can be solved without calling the function
	- and there is a recursive case
	  the piece that is solved by the fn calling itself
	  
---
JOVE copy multiple lines from one buffer to another
- set-mark at the beginning: ^Space
- go to the last line you want to be copied
- M-w to copy
- in the other buffer ^Y to yank the text in
---
JOVE delete a section
- set-mark: ^Space (beginning)
- cut: ^W
- then you can yank it with ^y
